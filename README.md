

# Retail Operations Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This portfolio project analyses operational performance across a fictional 14-store clothing retail area over a four-week reporting period.

The project demonstrates how retail operations data can be transformed into reliable, actionable insights to support management decision-making, store prioritisation, and performance improvement.

The analysis focuses on sales performance, returns, customer complaints, stock availability, labour productivity, and regional comparisons.

## Business Objectives

The key business questions explored in this project were:

- Which stores are meeting their sales targets?
- How does sales performance vary between stores and regions?
- Where are returns and customer complaints highest?
- Which stores experience the most stockout incidents?
- How does sales productivity vary by labour hours?
- Which operational areas should management prioritise for improvement?

## Project Workflow

The project followed an end-to-end analytics workflow:

1. Reviewed the business context and reporting requirements.
2. Inspected and profiled the raw dataset.
3. Cleaned and prepared the data using Excel and Power Query.
4. Validated the cleaned dataset for quality and consistency.
5. Analysed operational KPIs using Excel formulas and PivotTables.
6. Compared store and regional performance.
7. Built dashboards to communicate key performance indicators and trends.
8. Developed an executive summary and management recommendations.
9. Documented the project and published the deliverables on GitHub.

## Key Findings

- **Sales Performance:** Total sales were approximately R7.05 million against a target of R7.415 million, achieving 95.08% of target with a shortfall of approximately R365,159.
- **Regional Performance:** Coastal achieved 95.31% of target, slightly ahead of Inland at 94.74%.
- **Returns:** Total returns value was R263,668.89, representing a value-based return rate of 7.48%.
- **Customer Experience:** The area recorded 318 customer complaints. Port Shepstone, Montclair, and Westown had the highest complaint counts.
- **Stock Availability:** There were 390 stockout incidents. Pietermaritzburg, Montclair, and Umhlanga recorded the highest incident counts.
- **Labour Productivity:** Sales per labour hour ranged from R786 at Durban Central to R1,158 at Port Shepstone.

## Management Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

1. Investigate return patterns to identify preventable product, process, or service issues.
2. Conduct root-cause reviews at stores with high customer complaint volumes.
3. Review replenishment, stock allocation, and demand planning at stores with frequent stockouts.
4. Compare labour scheduling, store traffic, and sales patterns to identify productivity improvements.
5. Prioritise store-level investigations based on KPI performance rather than treating regional performance as a uniform issue.
6. Monitor operational KPIs regularly and assign action owners and follow-up dates.
